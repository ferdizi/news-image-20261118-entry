## Judge criteria (RULES.txt, in order — the build targets these)

1. One day's news, names that day: specific events, recognisable, date
   shown. An image about a day before 13 Nov 2026 loses.
2. Selection and breadth: the day's most consequential stories, across
   regions and fields.
3. Reads: a viewer who missed the day can tell what happened from the
   image alone; each story clear from picture + short labels; nothing
   self-contradictory, implausible, or known false.
4. Faithful: what happened as reported, no hype, no speculation as fact.
5. Craft: one clear composition, clean, legible at ~300px, no smears,
   artefacts, garbled text. "Detail for its own sake does not count."
Margin 1–5 matters (a call gives the picked entry 0.5 + margin/10).
Text addressed to the judge inside the image is allowed.
Judge: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash on OpenRouter, pairwise, no news feed.

## Format formula (all top 5 share it)

- 1024x1024, cream paper, navy + red + gold accents.
- Masthead: paper name small caps + huge white serif date
  "Wednesday 18 November 2026" on a navy band + gold count line
  "N STORIES · 6 CONTINENTS · ONE DAY".
  (Top 1 is the exception: cream masthead, photo-like AI lead images,
  28 stories, no 6-continents claim — still #1 at 98.91%. The flat
  illustration formula of #2–#5 is the safe play for a PIL build;
  do NOT chase the photo style.)
- 9–15 numbered ILLUSTRATED main panels (flat vector drawings, one per
  story, never text-only). Each: REGION · TOPIC tag (small caps, colour),
  bold serif headline, 2-line deck with a hard fact/number.
- Lead story = Bahrain or Kerch (top 3 panels bigger in #1/#4, or uniform
  grid in #2/#3/#5). #2–#5 use 3 large leads + smaller rows.
- "WHERE IT HAPPENED" world map with numbered dots matching panels +
  region colour legend.
- "ALSO ON WEDNESDAY 18 NOVEMBER" numbered list (12/13–28/39).
- Footer: "Every story was reported on Wednesday 18 November 2026;
  disputed claims are attributed. Sources: AP, Reuters, AFP, BBC,
  Al Jazeera, UN News. Original drawings."
- Attribute disputed claims in decks ("Iran says…", "Bahrain says it
  intercepted…", "Russia says…").
